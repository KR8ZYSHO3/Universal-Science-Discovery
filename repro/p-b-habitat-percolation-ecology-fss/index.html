Finite-size scaling test — does the 2D site percolation threshold shift with lattice area as p_c(L) = p_c(inf) + c * L^(-1/nu) with nu = 4/3?

Protocol p-b-habitat-percolation-ecology-fss · bridge b-habitat-percolation-ecology

2D site percolation, periodic 4-neighbor square lattice, Newman–Ziff; p_c(L) is the mean occupation fraction at first wrapping in either direction. That observable obeys p_c(L) = p_c(inf) + c L^(-1/nu) with p_c(inf) = 0.59274621. A weighted fit on L ∈ {32, 64, 128, 256} recovers nu within 15% of 4/3, with reported SE(nu) inside that tolerance.
